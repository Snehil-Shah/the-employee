# Proxy detection

A proxy is wording that reads as behaviour but selects for a group. For each
proposed trait, check it against the patterns below. Then ask: *could a
strong performer fail this description because of who they are or their
circumstances, rather than how they work?*

| Pattern | Examples | Who it excludes | Keep the behaviour by saying |
|---|---|---|---|
| Availability | "always reachable", "works late", "weekends when needed" | Caregivers, people with disabilities, religious observance | "responds within the agreed on-call window", "plans ahead so crunch is rare" |
| Age-coding | "young", "fresh", "digital native", "high energy", "recent grad" | Older workers | "learns new tools quickly", "brings momentum to stalled work" |
| Pedigree | "top-tier college", "IIT/Ivy", "big-name company" | Lower-income backgrounds, self-taught people | the actual skill: "designs systems that hold up at scale" |
| Language & accent | "native speaker", "polished English", "no accent" | Non-native speakers, regional backgrounds | "explains decisions clearly in writing" |
| Gender-coded | "aggressive", "dominant", "rockstar", "ninja", "nurturing" | Skews applicants and evaluators by gender | "pushes back with evidence", "supports teammates' growth" |
| Culture fit | "fits in", "one of us", "beer with" | Anyone unlike the current team | name the value: "disagrees openly, then commits" |
| Physical & location | "able-bodied", "lives nearby", "in office daily" (when not essential) | People with disabilities, people who can't relocate | state the real constraint only if it's essential to the job |
| Life stage | "no commitments", "can relocate anytime", "settled" | Parents, people with family obligations | state the actual requirement |
| Employment gaps | "continuous track record", "no gaps" | Caregivers, people who had illness or layoffs | "shows recent depth in X" |

## Interviewer reasons

Reasons for a rejection hide proxies more often than trait text does,
because they're written quickly and about a specific person. Watch for:

| Reason as written | What it can really mean | Ask for |
|---|---|---|
| "communication wasn't there", "not articulate" | Accent, non-native fluency, nervousness | What did they fail to explain, and when? |
| "lacked presence", "not confident", "too quiet" | Introversion, gender or cultural norms of self-promotion | What decision or argument did they not make? |
| "not a culture fit", "wouldn't vibe with the team" | Unlike the current team | Which named trait did they miss? |
| "low energy", "not hungry enough" | Age, disability, illness, a bad day | What behaviour, specifically? |
| "overqualified", "won't stay" | Age, career gaps, life stage | Not a behaviour. Don't build on it. |

If two interviewers agree but both reasons are proxies, the agreement
doesn't fix it. Flag it.

## Recording a verdict

- `clear`: no proxy found. Give a one-sentence summary.
- `flagged`: list each concern with the exact `term`, `why` (who it
  excludes) and a `suggestion`.

Also flag a change whose evidence is one person's personal circumstance
rather than a pattern in how work gets done here.
